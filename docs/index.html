How to read this

Pins: filled dot = pin up, seen from the front (UL UR / DL DR).

Front wheel: turn any wheel whose pin is up. Back wheel: turn any wheel whose pin is down. Do both without changing pins.

All turns are hours as seen from the front: 3+ is three hours clockwise, 2- two hours anticlockwise.

The small formula under each turn is what you would memorise to compute it from the scrambled faces (sum the listed clocks, then reduce to −5…6). Turns tagged intuitive can be done by eye: just line the clocks up.
