How to read this

Pins: filled dot = pin up, seen from the front (UL UR / DL DR).

Front move: turn any wheel whose pin is up. Back move: turn any wheel whose pin is down. Do both without changing pins.

All turns are hours as seen from the front: 3+ is three hours clockwise, 2- two hours anticlockwise.

The small formula under each turn is what you would memorize to compute it from the scrambled faces (sum the listed clocks, then reduce to −5…6). Turns tagged intuitive are done by eye: the text under them says which clocks to line up with which (e.g. align C to U/L means turn until C shows the same hour as U and L; a block is a group of clocks that already agree). The last pin state always just aligns everything to 12.
